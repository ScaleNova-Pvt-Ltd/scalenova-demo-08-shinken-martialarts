Dojo Lineage & Creed

Founded in 2008 by Kyoshi Kenji Tanaka (8th Dan), Shinken Karate maintains strict allegiance to the classic Okinawan karate philosophy: Karate ni sente nashi (There is no first strike in karate). True victory is conquering one's own limitations.

The Five Dojo Kun (Virtues)

  • Seek perfection of character.
  • Be faithful and loyal.
  • Endeavor and persevere.
  • Respect others.
  • Refrain from violent behavior.
